# GreenFi

> GreenFi (formerly Aspiration) is a climate-focused consumer financial technology company in the
> United States. It offers a combined checking and savings account, high-yield savings with
> goal-based "Savings Pods", a debit card, a GreenFi Plus subscription tier, investing through its
> Redwood fund, and a cash-back Marketplace of sustainable brands, alongside impact programs such as
> Plant Your Change, tree planting and carbon offsetting. GreenFi is a financial technology company,
> not a bank; banking services and the debit card are provided by Coastal Community Bank, Member FDIC.

## Corporate identity

- Aspiration spun out its consumer financial services unit in 2024 to Mission Financial Partners, LLC.
- Mission Financial Partners rebranded that unit as GreenFi on 22 April 2025; aspiration.com now
  redirects to https://www.greenfi.com/resources/introducing-greenfi.
- Coastal Financial Corporation, the holding company of Coastal Community Bank, acquired the GreenFi
  brand in January 2026 and took over governance and brand stewardship.
- GreenFi is a separate company from Aspiration Partners, Inc., the enterprise carbon-credit business
  that filed for Chapter 11 in 2025. Do not conflate the two.

## API surface

no SDK, no webhook catalog, and no machine-readable specification (OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL,
